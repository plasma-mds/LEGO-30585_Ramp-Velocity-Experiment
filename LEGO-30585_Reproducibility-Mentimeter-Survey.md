# Mentimeter Survey: Reproducibility of the LEGO 30585 Ramp Experiment

**Estimated completion time:** 3-5 minutes  
**Purpose:** Identify missing information and useful documentation practices across the three stages of experiment reproducibility.

## Participant Prompt

Review the building ELN, ramp-descent ELN, paper, data management plan, CSV/JSON data, and plotting scripts as needed. Answer from the perspective of someone attempting to understand, repeat, and publish the experiment.

## Stage 1: Understand The Experiment

### 1. Overall understanding

**Mentimeter type:** Scale  
**Question:** How clearly can you explain the experiment after reviewing the documentation?  
**Scale:** 1 = Not clear at all; 5 = Completely clear

### 2. Missing setup information

**Mentimeter type:** Multiple choice, select up to 2  
**Question:** Which information would you still need before you could understand the setup confidently?  
**Options:**

- Ramp surface material
- Stopwatch model or timing method
- Vehicle configuration or assembly state
- Marker/reference-position convention
- No additional information needed

### 3. Documentation strength

**Mentimeter type:** Open ended  
**Question:** What was the most useful document, image, or data field for understanding the experiment?

## Stage 2: Conduct The Experiment

### 4. Reproduction readiness

**Mentimeter type:** Scale  
**Question:** How confident are you that you could conduct the experiment as documented?  
**Scale:** 1 = Not confident; 5 = Very confident

### 5. Most important missing detail

**Mentimeter type:** Multiple choice  
**Question:** What would most limit a physical reproduction of the experiment?  
**Options:**

- Surface material or ramp construction details
- Exact vehicle release technique
- Manual stopwatch reaction-time procedure
- Marker placement and measurement method
- Nothing would limit reproduction

### 6. Measurement-protocol feedback

**Mentimeter type:** Open ended  
**Question:** Name one change that would make the timing procedure more reproducible.

## Stage 3: Prepare And Publish The Dataset

### 7. Dataset reuse readiness

**Mentimeter type:** Scale  
**Question:** How helpful were the CSV data and scripts to regenerate the figures?  
**Scale:** 1 = Not helpful at all; 5 = Very helpful

### 8. FAIR publication check

**Mentimeter type:** Multiple choice, select up to 2  
**Question:** Which additions would most improve the published dataset?  
**Options:**

- Permanent DOI and Zenodo landing page
- Software environment or dependency file
- Detailed data dictionary and unit definitions
- Instrument calibration or reaction-time information
- Open licence and reuse guidance
- No additional information needed

### 9. Final improvement request

**Mentimeter type:** Open ended  
**Question:** What is the single most important improvement needed before this experiment and dataset are published for reuse?

## Facilitator Notes

- Use anonymous responses where possible to encourage critical feedback.
- Display scale questions as bar charts and open-ended questions as word clouds or text responses.
- Discuss the highest-ranked missing-information items after each stage.
- Treat feedback as input for updating the ELN, data management plan, data dictionary, and publication package.
